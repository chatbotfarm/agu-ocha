YOUR INFORMATION

A little clarity.

Here’s how the information you share helps Agu Ocha handle your inquiry.

What you share

Inquiry forms ask for contact details and information about your event, idea or act. Name or artist name, email and phone are required. Other required fields are marked on the form; optional links and social handles are your choice.

How it is used

Agu Ocha uses these details to review your request, ask relevant questions and follow up about it. Artist interest can also be reviewed when a relevant opportunity becomes available.

Where it goes

The forms are hosted by HighLevel (GHL), where Agu Ocha stores and manages inquiries.

Questions about your information

To ask about the details you shared or request a correction or deletion, contact djaguocha@gmail.com. Please don’t send payment details, passwords or sensitive documents through an inquiry form.
