YOUR INFORMATION

A little clarity.

What this site collects, what you send me, and how to ask about it.

What the site itself collects

Nothing. The pages are static: no analytics, advertising trackers or marketing pixels, and no cookies of their own. On the track submission page, a link you paste is held in your browser’s session storage so the form can fill it in for you, and that page notes which buttons and fields you used, in memory only, to spot problems. Neither is sent anywhere, and both are gone when you close the tab.

What you send me

The event inquiry, artist, media request and track submission forms, and the booking calendar, are hosted by HighLevel (GoHighLevel / LeadConnector), where I keep and manage what you send. Name or artist name, email and phone are required; other required fields are marked on each form. I use your details to reply, to set up and hold a call or review, and to keep a record. Artist interest may be reviewed again when a relevant opportunity comes up. I don’t sell your information.

Embedded players and forms

Forms, the calendar and the Spotify and YouTube players come from those providers. When one loads, that provider receives your request and applies its own privacy policy and cookies. Submitted tracks are handled under the song submission terms; please send public links, not file attachments.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log request details such as IP addresses for security under GitHub’s own policies. No form submissions are stored there.

Questions about your information

To see, correct or delete what you shared, email djaguocha@gmail.com. To withdraw a track submission, follow the submission terms. Please don’t send payment details, passwords or sensitive documents through a form.
