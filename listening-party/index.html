How the night works.

  1. Before

    I make the flyer and a video invite, so your guests know it’s your night.

    No logo or merch art yet? I can design it as an add-on.

  2. The night

    I put your logos and merch art up on the projectors and the venue’s screens, set out your merch table, and set up the lights and DJ rig.

  3. After

    I edit the footage, then post it across my channels on a schedule we agree, with your OK on every post.

    The footage is yours, and you keep your rights and your merch income.
