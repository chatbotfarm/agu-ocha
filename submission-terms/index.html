  • Mix a selected track into a DJ set using ordinary DJ technique — fades, crossfades, level adjustment, cueing, looping, and brief overlap with other tracks — as needed for a performance or a continuous mix.

Agu Ocha is not buying exclusive rights. You stay free to release, distribute, and promote your track anywhere else, at any time, including while it appears in a playlist or set.

If we ever need something beyond the permissions above, or need more information before publishing, we will contact you first.

4. No guarantee, and nothing for sale

Submission does not guarantee selection or placement. Selection is entirely at Agu Ocha’s discretion. Tracks are reviewed for upcoming playlists and DJ sets.

To be straightforward about what you can expect: if your track is selected, we will contact you within seven days. We cannot reply to every submission, so no reply is not a rejection notice — it usually means the track was not a fit for the sets being built that month. You are welcome to submit again next month.

No payment is required for submission or for placement, and placement cannot be purchased. We may add, reorder, or remove any track from a playlist, and may remove or unpublish a track or a DJ set, at our discretion.

5. Withdrawal

You may withdraw your submission at any time by texting +1 (762) 248-6242. We will remove the track from playlists we control within a reasonable period, and we will not include it in any new DJ set.

One practical limit worth stating plainly: a DJ set that has already been published as a single continuous recording cannot have one track lifted out of it. Where that is the case we will unpublish or replace the set instead. Removal from third-party platforms may take additional time to propagate.

6. Submissions are not confidential

Please do not send material you consider confidential. Submissions are not treated as confidential and create no confidential relationship. We may independently be creating, reviewing, or releasing work that resembles yours.

7. Eligibility and provenance

You must be at least 13 years old, or the age of majority where you live, to submit. If your track was generated or assisted with an AI tool, you are responsible for confirming that the tool’s terms permit you to distribute it and to give the permissions in section 3.

8. Changes

We may update these terms. The version published here at the time you submit is the version that applies to that submission.
