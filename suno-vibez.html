How submissions work right now

Submissions are reviewed on a short scheduled listening call. Pick a time below and bring a public link to your track (Spotify, SoundCloud, YouTube, or a direct stream link). We listen, log it, and it enters the next monthly playlist review. Submission does not guarantee playlist placement.

Prefer not to book a call? Text your track link to +1 (762) 248-6242 and it goes into the same queue.
