What we don’t do

  • We don’t guarantee placement. Selection is a judgement call, and most tracks don’t make it.
  • We don’t charge for submission, review, or placement — and there’s no paid fast lane.
  • We don’t buy streams, run stream-swaps, or ask you to stream other people’s tracks.
  • We don’t sell or share your email. One confirmation, one decision, and a monthly playlist email you can leave any time.
  • We don’t require a paid Suno plan to submit.

What we accept

Lane A — a Suno link (open to everyone)

  • Any track hosted on Suno, any subscription tier including free
  • Made by you
  • Released or unreleased, either is fine
  • Selected tracks join the Suno Vibez playlist on Suno

Lane B — a released track (Spotify, Apple Music and similar)

  • The track is live on streaming and you hold the rights
  • Generated while you were on a Suno Pro or Premier plan — the free tier doesn’t grant commercial rights, so free-tier tracks can’t be distributed
  • AI involvement disclosed with your distributor
  • Selected tracks join the Suno Vibez playlist on Spotify

Not sure which applies? Paste your link and we’ll work it out. Read the questions first if you’d rather.

What we can’t accept, in either lane

  • Vocals imitating a real, identifiable artist without their permission
  • Tracks you didn’t make, or that use samples you don’t have rights to
  • Hate speech, harassment, or sexual content involving minors
  • Bulk submissions — one track per creator per month
